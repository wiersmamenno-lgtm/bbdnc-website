about

Music, imagery and digital world-building.

bbdnc blends electronic pop, rap and travel-inspired storytelling. The project is created by wehome and brought to life through the fictional performers Anna Vaelmont and MAN1C.

The songs combine human songwriting, visual storytelling and AI-assisted creative tools — not as a gimmick, but as a way to build a complete artistic world.
